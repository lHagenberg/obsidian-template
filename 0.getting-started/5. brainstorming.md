This is the part that can be the most useful, but also most dependent on the way you organize your notes.
The notes you take can all be connected using some of the tools outlined earlier.
I myself am still figuring out how to do this in a way that makes sense for me.
# categorizing and connecting
Notes can be categorized using tags in the frontmatter or in text with hashtags, and directly linked using links.
This connects notes together in the obsidian.
These connections can be useful for finding notes and connecting information.
This supposedly allows you to create connections in your head and expand on ideas using the "zettlekasten" method (a word that I cannot stand for whatever reason).
Trust me, it is a real concept that you can look up on the internet.

This vault has two folders for notes (3.notes and 4.zettle).
I use the notes folder for messy notes that are not clearly connected or are just long text, while I use the zettle folder for short more atomic notes that contain singular concepts.
Feel free to change these two folders as you like.
