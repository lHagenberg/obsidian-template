obsidian is a note taking tool that I have been personalizing to assist me in [[3. literature work flow|reading and storing knowledge]], [[4. planning and keeping track of information|planning and organizing]], [[5. brainstorming|brainstorming]], [[6. more|more]].

- [P] why it works for me:
    - it can organize messy (non-linear) thoughts
    - I do not need to remember things
    - it's very visual (if you make it so)
    - it's interesting to learn
    - it's a great way to procrastinate towards something useful
    - it's highly customizable (you can do some crazy stuff if you can think of it)
    - everything is locally on your computer (no cloud bullshit)
    - it's completely offline (if you want it to be)

- [C] why it (initially) didn't work for me:
    - it's a lot to get into
    - I needed to get used to using it
    - I needed to make it work for me
    - it does not stop you from creating a hopeless, useless mess
    - it can be a huge rabbit hole
