**The N cycle is the conversion and use of different chemical forms of nitrogen.**
N can be be present in the form of [[organic N]] and [[inorganic N]].

In terrestrial systems, the only external source of N is atmospheric nitrogen derived from [[N fixation]].
N already present in the soil gets used and reused by different organisms through the processes of [[N immobilization]], [[nitrification]], and [[N mineralization]], respectively.
[[litter CN ratio determines the fate of decomposed N|litter CN ratio influences the rates of mineralization vs immobiliazation]].
[[N mineralization and nitrification are temporally distinct processes]].

Depending on the context, [[how open is the N cycle|the soil N cycle can be more open or more closed]].
