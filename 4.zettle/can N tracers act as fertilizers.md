- [C] tracer addition may have acted as pulse fertilization
- [P] microbes probably took up most of the tracer before other organisms can access it (it did not have a chance to act as a fertilizer for plants in the experiment).
N tracer quickly gets integrated into the [[soil N cycle]] without acting as a fertilizer as long as it does not significantly alter ambient soil N concentrations.
