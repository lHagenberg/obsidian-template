The rates of [[N mineralization]] and [[N immobilization]] by soil microbes are highly dependent on CN ratio of decomposing material. 
When the CN ratio is above 30:1 (i.e., N is scarce), microbes may take up more [[inorganic N]], immediately immobilizing the product of mineralization.
When the CN ratio is below 25:1 (i.e., N is abundant), decomposition results in [[N mineralization|mineralization of excess N]].
- [*] Thus, [[litter quality]] and soil legacy determine mineralization rates.
