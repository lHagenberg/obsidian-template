*!* **Eri/ectoM and non/arbM plants use distinct N sources.** [[@michelsen1996]]
    - eri and ectoM plants rely on [[organic N]] (*i.e., litter derived*).
        - Ecto- and eriM species take up more amino acids than non-mycorrhizal species. 
    - nonM and arbM species rely on [[inorganic N]].
