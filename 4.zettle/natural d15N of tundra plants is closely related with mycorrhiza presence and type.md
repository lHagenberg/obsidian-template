plants with lower d15N (eri lowest, ecto middle, non highest):
ectoM and and eriM are able to mobilize N originating from organic substrates (which have higher 15N).
- immobilization of N by mycorrhiza depletes 15N (15N is discriminated against)
Yet, d15N is more depleted than that of non/arbM plants. [[@michelsen1996]]

 microbial N is not accessible to arb/nonM plants, leading to higher d15N in arb/nonM plants in comparison.
