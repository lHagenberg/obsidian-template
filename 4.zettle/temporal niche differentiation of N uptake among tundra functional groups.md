**patterns of N uptake by tundra plant functional groups are temporally distinct**
- [!] arctic plants can take up N starting from late winter and early spring
- evergreen dwarf shrubs took up N between early April and May (in sub-zero soils).
- [!] post-snowmelt is an important period for nutrient uptake by evergreen dwarf shrubs
    - N is quickly allocated to aboveground tissues
- N uptake and allocation in deciduous dwarf shrubs happened one month later
- graminoids experience a peak in N uptake during the fall
- early onset of N uptake by evergreens may compensate for comparatively slower growth rates during summer
