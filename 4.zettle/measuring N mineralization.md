(Net) [[N mineralization]] can be measured as the difference between NH4+ and NO3- in the same soil.
This is under the assumption that the main source of NH4+ is decomposition of organic matter, the main source of NH3 is [[N fixation]], and the main source of NO3- is [[nitrification]]
